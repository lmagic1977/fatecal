# FateCal: how we make money without losing trust

FateCal earns from affiliate links (flights, hotels, products), subscriptions and, later, its own products.
These rules apply to every screen, every FateBot answer, every email and every notification.
Anyone working on FateCal (people or AI assistants) should follow them. If a feature needs to break one, ask Jason first.

## 1. Advice first, the sale second
- Every "not good" reading starts with what the person can do for free: pick another time, face a direction, wear a colour, prepare well.
- Products come after that, as an option. Never as the only answer.
- Show remedies and products only when something really scores "not good" (or the person asks). A good day gets no sales pitch.

## 2. No fear, no guarantees
- Say "traditionally, people…" or "this can help you feel prepared". Never "you will have an accident / lose money unless…".
- No object, amulet or purchase is presented as guaranteeing an outcome. No health or safety claims.
- No urgency tricks: no countdowns, "only 2 left", "act today or…".
- Bad readings are never pushed as notifications just to sell something.

## 3. Always honest about money
- Every affiliate link is marked as one ("affiliate link" / "联盟链接") right next to it, and the Tools & affiliate disclosure page stays linked from every footer (FTC rules).
- At most three products per suggestion. Real products only; no fake reviews, no invented discounts.
- The score is never changed to sell something. The rules engine decides the reading; commercial partners never influence it.

## 4. Subscriptions people don't regret
- Free tier stays genuinely useful: today's score, the month calendar, the chart and personal directions.
- Trials say clearly when they end and what they cost, with a reminder before the first charge.
- Cancelling takes one tap in Settings. No hiding it, no guilt screens.
- Refunds for honest mistakes (forgot to cancel the day after) are granted.

## 5. The person decides
- FateCal suggests; it never moves a real meeting, buys anything or contacts anyone without the person doing it.
- Big decisions (money, health, legal, relationships): FateCal reminds people to weigh the practical facts first. The almanac is one input.

## Checklist before shipping anything that sells
- [ ] Is there a free option shown first?
- [ ] Is the wording calm, with no fear and no guarantee?
- [ ] Is every affiliate link labelled?
- [ ] Would the person feel fine about this if they saw how it works?
